English

Palabrio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no internet access.

What we collect

Nothing. Palabrio does not gather personal information of any kind — no name, no email, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, and no usage analytics.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally in your device's private app storage so you can pick up where you left off. This includes:

  • Puzzle progress and results for the current day
  • Your streaks, win counts and unlocked achievements
  • Your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, high contrast)
  • Any custom words you add yourself

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Internet access

Palabrio is fully offline. The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending or receiving data over a network.

Permissions we request

  • Vibration — used only for tactile feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.

No other permissions are requested.

Children

Palabrio is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics providers, or crash reporters in the app. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Palabrio ever adds a feature that handles data (for example an optional online mode), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
