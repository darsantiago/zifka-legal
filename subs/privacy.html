Privacy Policy

Zifka Subs — Last updated: July 11, 2026

Summary

This app is offline-first. It does not require an account, and the subscription data you enter never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Subscription data: services, prices, renewal dates, categories, and notes you enter
  • Price history you record for each service
  • Preferences: language, theme, and reminder settings

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, location, or analytics data.

How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used only inside the app, on your device, to show your subscriptions, calendar, forecasts, and insights; to schedule the local reminders you enable; and to generate exports (CSV/PDF) that you choose to share. Nothing is sent to us or used for advertising or profiling.

Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored locally on your device
  • You can protect the app with biometric lock (fingerprint / Face ID)
  • Backup files you create are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and remain under your control

Zifka Ecosystem (optional)

If you install other Zifka apps (Zifka Budget, Zifka Habits) and enable the ecosystem connection, summary totals (such as your recurring monthly total) are shared between the apps on your own device through an encrypted local vault. This data never leaves your device, and app-to-app access is verified by signing certificate.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Exports or backups you explicitly create and share
  • Purchase information processed by Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat when you buy a subscription (an anonymous purchase identifier — never your subscription data)

Device Permissions

  • Notifications: only to deliver the renewal reminders you schedule, entirely on-device
  • Biometrics: only to lock and unlock the app

All permissions are optional and requested only when you use the related feature.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and edit all of your data inside the app
  • Export your data in CSV or PDF format at any time
  • Delete all data permanently from Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@zifka.com.
