1. Controller

LUXCADE is a suite of 14 arcade games, including three originals, developed and published by Saribot SAS, a company registered in Colombia (DUNS 686545181), operating under the brand "LUXCADE" (referred to as "we", "us", or "the app" in this policy). We respect your privacy, and this policy explains what data the app handles and how.

For any privacy-related request, write to info@sari-ai.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Create (stored only on your device)

  • High scores and records for each game.
  • Games played, achievements unlocked, and progress statistics.
  • Preferences (language, difficulty, sound, vibration, theme).

All of this lives in the app's private storage and is not transmitted to Saribot SAS.

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, account, or user identifiers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not transmit your game data off the device; scores, progress, and settings stay in the app's local storage. (Subscription verification through Google Play is described in section 2.3.)
  • We do not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics services.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

2.3 Subscriptions through Google Play

LUXCADE offers monthly and annual plans, with a free trial when available in the Google Play offer. Google processes payment and account data under its own privacy policy. The app receives only purchase status and the technical information needed to grant or restore access; Saribot SAS does not receive your banking details.

3. Device Permissions

LUXCADE uses:

  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in some games (for example, collisions or game over). Can be turned off in the Settings screen.
  • Network connectivity: only through Google Play services to query and verify subscriptions. This does not require an Android runtime permission prompt.

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, external storage, notifications, or contacts.

4. Storage and Retention

  • All data is stored locally in the app's private storage.
  • Data remains while the app is installed. Uninstalling the app removes it completely from the device.
  • You can wipe progress from Settings → "Reset data" at any time.

5. Children

LUXCADE is suitable for all ages. It contains no advertising, chat, or behavioral tracking. Purchases are handled through Google Play's family and authentication controls. Parents can erase progress from Settings or uninstall the app.

6. Your Rights

Saribot SAS does not maintain a player account or player database. For purchase questions or rights related to data processed by Google Play, use your Google Account controls or write to info@sari-ai.com.
