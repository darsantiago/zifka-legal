Privacy Policy

Zifka Budget — Last updated: July 6, 2026

Summary

This app is offline-first. It does not require an account, and the financial data you enter never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Financial data: transactions, budgets, goals, and accounts you enter
  • Preferences: language, theme, and notification settings

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, location, or analytics data.

How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used only inside the app, on your device, to show your transactions, budgets, goals, and insights; to schedule local reminders you enable; and to generate exports (CSV/PDF) that you choose to share. Nothing is sent to us or used for advertising or profiling.

Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored locally in encrypted databases (AES-256)
  • You can protect the app with biometric lock (fingerprint / Face ID)
  • Backup files you create are encrypted and remain under your control

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Exports or backups you explicitly create and share
  • Purchase information processed by Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat when you buy a subscription (an anonymous purchase identifier — never your financial data)

Device Permissions

  • Camera / Photos: only to attach receipt images to transactions
  • Microphone / Speech recognition: only for voice input of transactions, processed on-device
  • Biometrics: only to lock and unlock the app

All permissions are optional and requested only when you use the related feature.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and edit all of your data inside the app
  • Export your data in CSV or PDF format at any time
  • Delete all data permanently from Settings → Delete All Data
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@zifka.com.
