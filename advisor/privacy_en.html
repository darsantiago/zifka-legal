Privacy Policy

Zifka Advisor (Financial Calculator Pro) — Last updated: February 4, 2026

Saribot SAS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Financial Calculator Pro / Zifka Advisor mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

Summary: We do not collect any personal data. Everything stays on your device. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. GDPR and CCPA compliant.

1. Information Collection and Use

1.1 Data We DO NOT Collect

Zifka Advisor is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number).
  • Financial account information (bank accounts, credit cards).
  • Location data.
  • Usage analytics or tracking data.
  • Device identifiers for advertising.
  • Browsing history or search queries.

1.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Calculation history: your past calculations and saved results.
  • User preferences: app settings (theme, default currency, etc.).
  • Pro features status: whether you have activated premium features.

Storage uses Hive (local encrypted database) and Shared Preferences. All data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or any third-party service.

1.3 Currency Exchange Rates (optional feature)

If you use the Currency Converter feature, the App may make network requests to public currency exchange rate APIs (e.g. exchangerate-api.com, fixer.io). These requests only transmit currency codes (e.g. "USD to EUR"). No personal information is sent.

2. PDF Export and Sharing

  • PDFs are generated locally on your device.
  • Sharing uses your device's native share functionality (iOS / Android Share Sheet).
  • If you choose to share via email or messaging apps, those apps' privacy policies apply.
  • We do not have access to what you share or where you share it.

3. Permissions Requested

Android

  • INTERNET: required only for currency exchange rate updates (optional).
  • VIBRATE: for haptic feedback when pressing buttons (optional).

iOS

  • Photo Library access: only if you choose to save PDF reports to photos.
  • Network access: for currency exchange rate updates (optional).

All permissions are optional; the app functions fully without granting them.

4. Third-Party Services

Zifka Advisor does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App uses open-source libraries (Flutter, Riverpod, Hive, fl_chart) that do not collect data.

5. Children's Privacy

Zifka Advisor does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and does not require any personal information.

6. GDPR Compliance (European Union)

6.1 Your rights

  • Access: you can view all your data in the App's settings.
  • Rectification: you can edit or correct any saved data.
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten): delete all app data via Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Data portability: export your data as PDF reports.
  • Objection: you can disable any optional feature (currency converter, haptic feedback).

6.2 Legal basis for processing

  • Legitimate interest: storing calculation history to provide app functionality.
  • Consent: optional features require your explicit action.

6.3 Data retention

  • Data is stored indefinitely on your device until you delete it.
  • No data is stored on our servers.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

7. CCPA Compliance (California)

We do not sell personal information. The App does not collect personal information for sale, share it with third parties for monetary benefit, or use it for targeted advertising. All data is visible in the App; you can delete it via Settings; there is no data collection to opt out of; no features are gated by data sharing.

8. Data Security

  • Data is stored using Hive's encrypted storage.
  • Sensitive calculations can be protected by your device's lock screen.
  • No data is transmitted over networks, except the optional currency rates feature.
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption is used for all network requests.
  • No authentication tokens or API keys are stored locally.

9. International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device, no international data transfers occur. Your data never leaves your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be notified inside the app.
