1. Controller

SariGames is a suite of 11 classic arcade games developed and published by SariGames ("we" or "the app"). We respect your privacy, and this policy explains what data the app handles and how.

For any privacy-related request, write to privacy@sarigames.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Create (stored only on your device)

  • High scores and records for each game.
  • Games played, achievements unlocked, and progress statistics.
  • Preferences (language, difficulty, sound, vibration, theme).

All of this lives in a local savedata.json file inside the app's private storage. It never leaves your device.

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, account, or user identifiers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not connect to the internet: the app runs fully offline.
  • We do not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

3. Device Permissions

SariGames requests a single optional permission:

  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in some games (for example, collisions or game over). Can be turned off in the Settings screen.

The app does not request network, location, camera, microphone, external storage, notification, or contacts permissions.

4. Storage and Retention

  • All data is stored locally in the app's private storage.
  • Data remains while the app is installed. Uninstalling the app removes it completely from the device.
  • You can wipe progress from Settings → "Reset data" at any time.

5. Children

SariGames is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal data, no children's data is collected. Parents can uninstall the app at any time to remove all local progress.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect any personally identifying data, there is no information about you in our systems to request, rectify, or delete. If you have questions, write to privacy@sarigames.com.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version. The most recent version is always available at this page.

8. Contact
